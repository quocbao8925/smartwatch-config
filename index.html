🍅 POMODORO Setup

Step 1: Connect POMODORO to your computer via USB-C (or C-C).

Step 2: Click the "Connect Cable" button below (The device will briefly reboot due to USB safety mechanisms).

Step 3: After the screen turns back on, flip the device to the "CONFIG" screen and wait 5 seconds.

Step 4: Enter your WiFi credentials and click Sync.
